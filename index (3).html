// Code of Conduct

Clan Rules

Every member of CYBER SQURD is expected to hold these standards on and off the battlefield.

01

Respect the Squad

No toxicity, harassment, or disrespect toward teammates or opponents — in-game or in voice chat.

02

Show Up

Attend scheduled scrims and practice sessions. Notify leadership in advance if you can't make it.

03

No Cheating, Ever

Hacks, exploits, or third-party tools result in immediate removal from the clan.

04

Follow the IGL

During ranked pushes and tournaments, the IGL's call is final. Discuss strategy, not during the fight.

05

Represent the Crest

Keep the clan tag and name professional across your in-game ID and social profiles.

06

Growth Mindset

Review losses, share clips, and help lower-level members improve. We rank up together.
